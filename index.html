      How the page applies the overtime procedure
      • Next up ranks the lowest offered hours first (SOP 25a, 29, 35). Holiday shifts rank the highest worked hours first (SOP 25b).
      • Skipped for overtime: protected days off, light duty, personal time off (blue), vacation (yellow hatch), already working that day, less than 8 hours rest (SOP 24, 34), and anyone set as not available for overtime. The on-call person is shown in orange when they are otherwise available.
      • Sick call-outs go to the on-call person first (SOP 31). Unscheduled vacation does not offer to on call (SOP 29).
      • Anyone on personal time off (blue) or vacation (yellow hatch) that day is left off the Next up list.
      • A seventh day in a row is flagged as double time (SOP 34c).
      • Editing a cell warns about overtime that breaks a rule, more than 2 people off per day or per shift (SOP 2, 3), more than 1 person protected (SOP 18), fewer than 3 on a shift (SOP 1), offered totals that go down, and changes to posted overtime (SOP 22).
      • Warnings never block a save. Deviations from the call-out policy should be approved by management (SOP 32).
      • Not checked: vacation request deadlines (SOP 4 to 6), the on-call rotation (SOP 7 to 16) and hold-over length.
